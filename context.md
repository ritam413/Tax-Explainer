# Project Context: AI Tax / Budget Explainer

## Vision
Help citizens build and understand government budgets through an interactive, AI-powered visualizer — turning dense government PDFs into something a person can grasp in under 5 seconds.

## Target Users
- **Primary:** Students (~19 years old) wanting simple, fast explanations.
- **Secondary:** Journalists needing to compare budgets for stories.
- **Secondary:** Researchers wanting to export structured data.

## Solution
An interactive AI visualizer allowing users to search, explore, compare, and simulate government budgets, layered with plain-language AI explanations.

## MoSCoW Prioritization
- **Must Have:** Dashboard, Budget Search, AI Explain, Charts, Country Compare.
- **Should Have:** Bookmark, Dark Mode, Profile.
- **Could Have:** Feedback, Profile 3D Visualizer, Community, Voice Assistant.
- **Won't Have:** Multiplayer / Chat, Marketplace.

## Success Metrics
- Time-to-first-insight under 5 seconds.
- High percentage of sessions viewing charts or bookmarking.
- AI explanation satisfaction.
- 7-day retention.
